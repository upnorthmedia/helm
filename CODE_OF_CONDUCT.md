# Code of conduct

Helm is a small project, and we want it to be a good place to ask questions, report problems, and send changes, whatever your experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique code, designs, and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with questions, including ones that are answered somewhere already.
- Keep discussion on topic and in the open, unless it needs privacy.
- Accept a decision you disagree with once it is made, or make your case again with new information.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination or slurs about any person or group.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting issues, pull requests, or discussions.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and review comments, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private reporting form ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) explains where it is; say that it is a conduct report). You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block anyone from the project who does not follow this code, temporarily or permanently, and will say why when that is safe to do.

These expectations draw on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
